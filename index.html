Welcome to the team

Thanks for being part of our season. Most of our players are brand new to basketball, so our goals are simple: make friends, learn the basics, and have fun.

12 Tuesday practices starting Nov 3, and 8 Saturday games from Dec 5 to early February.

What we ask of families

  • Let a coach know 24 hours ahead if your player will miss a game, so playing time stays fair.
  • Drop off at 5:30 and pick up at 6:30. Doors can open a few minutes late.
  • Help with snacks and the game clock once or twice a season (see Duties).
  • Send any allergy or medical notes to Coach Brett privately.
  • Cheer for every player on both teams.

Want to help at practice? Extra hands are always welcome; just text Coach Brett. Game times and team updates will move to the league's TeamSideline app once it's ready. Everything else is under Schedule and Info.

Schedule

Practices Tuesdays 5:30–6:30 at Rockwell. Games Saturdays from Dec 5; times come from the league in mid-November. No practice when school is out.

How practices work: each week has one focus, and the skills build on each other through the season. We'll adjust as we go based on what the team needs.

Snacks & clock

How it works
  • Kari Shields is coordinating snack sign-ups; details coming soon.
  • Each family brings snacks for one game, for 8 players.
  • Keep it simple: a drink (water or juice boxes), fruit, and one small treat.
  • Nut-free, please, individually packaged if you can.
  • We hand snacks out outside the gym after the game; food isn't allowed on the court.
  • Can't make your game? Trade with another family and let Coach Brett know.
Clock / scorebook duty
  • The league needs one grown-up from our team at the scorer's table each game.
  • Each family takes one game, on a different week from their snack game.
  • Arrive 15 minutes early and check in at the scorer's table; the refs show you what to do. No experience needed.

Team info

What to bring
  • Gym shoes and comfortable athletic clothes
  • Hair tied back, no jewelry
  • A water bottle (water only in the gym)
  • Jersey on game days. Jerseys arrive mid-November; players keep them and their basketballs.
Practice and drop-off

Tuesdays 5:30–6:30 pm at Rockwell Elementary, 11125 162nd Ave NE, Redmond. Park in the front lot. The custodian unlocks around 5:30, so you may wait a few minutes. The league asks families to drop off or wait in the car; on the first night, please stay the first 5 minutes so we can all meet. Please pick up at 6:30.

Games

Saturdays, Dec 5 to early February, at Redmond Middle School or the Redmond Senior & Community Center. Arrive 15 minutes early. The league lists us as “Shields.” No bouncing balls in the lobby, and the community center's upstairs is off-limits.

Playing time and missing a game

The league's spirit is equal playing time. Five players play at once and we have eight, so we rotate every five minutes. No score or standings are kept at this age.

Missing a game? Please tell Coach Brett at least 24 hours ahead so the rotation stays fair. For practice, just a heads-up if your player will miss more than one.

Bad weather

The league posts game decisions by 7:30 am on game day at redmond.gov/sports. No practice when school is canceled.

From the sidelines

From the league's code of conduct: kids play to have fun, coaches are volunteers, officials are human, and parents are cheerleaders. Cheer for every player on both teams, and leave the coaching and the calls to the coaches and refs.
